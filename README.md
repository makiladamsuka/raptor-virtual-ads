# Raptor — Virtual Pitch-Side Advertising for Grassroots Sports

> **SpiritX 2.0** (*"Where Sports Meets Technology"* — Organized by MoraSpirit)

---

## What This Project Is About

**Raptor** is a virtual advertising and local sponsorship platform built for university and community sports livestreams.

It allows sports broadcasters to place virtual sponsor billboards directly onto the playing field or perimeter during a live match—without printing physical banners or buying expensive stadium equipment. Whenever the cameraman naturally points the camera at a chosen spot on the pitch, the scheduled sponsor's billboard appears seamlessly as part of the ground, blending with the natural lighting while players run in front of it.

At the same time, local businesses (like nearby cafés, bookshops, eateries, and tutoring centers) can visit an online dashboard, pick an upcoming match, book an affordable time slot, and have their advertisement automatically displayed during the live stream.

---

## The Current Situation

University and school sports in Sri Lanka—led by initiatives like **MoraSpirit**—command massive attention, covering hundreds of matches a year and reaching over a hundred thousand students, athletes, and alumni. Despite this huge audience, grassroots sports broadcasting faces several major hurdles:

1. **Broadcasts Are Expensive to Run, Yet Hard to Monetize:**
   Covering hundreds of live matches requires cameras, mobile data, equipment, and dedicated volunteer crews. Yet most university matches generate little to no on-ground advertising income.
2. **Physical Banners Are Costly and Wasteful:**
   Printing traditional PVC flex banners for every match is expensive, logistically difficult to transport between grounds, only visible to people physically at the venue, and creates large amounts of single-use plastic waste.
3. **Small Local Businesses Are Locked Out:**
   Full-tournament sponsorships are too expensive for small campus-adjacent shops, leaving them with no easy way to reach thousands of students watching matches online.
4. **Pro TV Virtual Ads Are Out of Reach:**
   The virtual grass ads seen on international cricket and football broadcasts require tens of thousands of dollars in specialized broadcast trucks, custom camera hardware, and dedicated operators—making them completely inaccessible to university media teams.
5. **No Proof of Value for Sponsors:**
   Even when small businesses do sponsor a university event, they rarely receive clear proof of how many people saw their brand or for how long.

---

## What We Are Going to Address

Raptor bridges the gap between grassroots sports media and local businesses by addressing four core needs:

1. **Zero-Waste, Zero-Banner Virtual Ad Placement:**
   Broadcasters simply set up a few camera views on the pitch before the match. During the live game, the cameraman films normally—whenever the camera points toward a configured view, the virtual billboard appears naturally on the field.
2. **Realistic Look Without Distorting Brands:**
   Virtual ads are adjusted to match the match's natural lighting and shadows so they look like part of the ground rather than a fake sticker, while still keeping sponsor logos and colors clear and recognizable.
3. **Affordable Self-Serve Time Slots for Local Shops:**
   Instead of expensive full-event contracts, local businesses can purchase short, budget-friendly time slots (such as 5-minute windows) through a simple self-serve dashboard. Shops without designers can also generate clean, readable billboards in seconds, and broadcasters retain full approval control before anything goes live.
4. **Fair Billing & Verified Proof of Visibility:**
   Rather than charging a shop even if the camera never pointed at their ad spot, Raptor tracks the exact time the billboard was actually visible on screen—giving local sponsors transparent, verifiable proof of their return on investment.

---

## Impact

- **For Broadcasters & Sports Councils (e.g., MoraSpirit):** Turns live university sports coverage from an ongoing expense into a self-funding broadcast model without disrupting how crews already work.
- **For Local Businesses:** Opens up affordable, high-visibility advertising to thousands of students and alumni during live matches.
- **For the Environment:** Eliminates the need for single-use printed PVC flex banners at university and school sporting events.
